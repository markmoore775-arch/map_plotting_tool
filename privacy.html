AirPlot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 April 2026

Summary

AirPlot runs mostly in your browser. Planning data, flight report drafts, checklist progress, and similar state are stored on your device unless you export or email them yourself. When you use maps, weather, or traffic features, third-party services may receive technical requests (for example map tiles or API calls) as described below.

Who we are

AirPlot is provided by M Moore. For privacy questions, feedback, or data requests: support@airplot.app.

Information you send to us

If you email support@airplot.app, we receive your email address, message content, and message headers your provider includes. We use this only to respond and improve the service, and we do not sell your personal information.

Data stored on your device

Features such as the planning map, flight report, checklist, and weather tools may save data in your browser (for example local storage or session storage) so your work persists between visits. This data stays on your machine until you clear it, use in-app clear actions, or remove site data in your browser settings. We do not receive that data automatically.

API keys and configuration

Optional services (for example Mapbox, What3Words, or NATS-related airspace credentials) may ask you to enter keys or passwords in Settings or in js/config.js. Those values are used from your browser to call the relevant providers; treat them as sensitive and do not share them.

Third-party services

Depending on which tools you use, your browser may load resources or send requests to third parties, including but not limited to:

  • Maps and location: OpenStreetMap and other tile providers; optional Mapbox or Ordnance Survey when configured; Leaflet and related libraries (often via CDNs such as unpkg or jsDelivr).
  • Weather and aviation data: Open-Meteo and other forecast sources; official or third-party airspace and NOTAM-related endpoints where the app is configured to use them.
  • Traffic awareness: ADS-B and glider-network style feeds (for example ADSB.lol, Open Glider Network) when you open Airspace-related views.
  • Fonts and assets: Google Fonts or similar CDNs when linked from pages.

Each provider has its own privacy policy governing how they process requests and logs. Use of those features is optional and under your control.

Cookies

AirPlot does not use tracking cookies for advertising. Your browser may still store small amounts of data for essential site behaviour (for example install prompts or preferences). Third-party embeds may set their own cookies according to their policies.

Analytics

This policy covers the AirPlot site as published. If first-party or third-party analytics are added later, this page will be updated to name the provider and what is collected.

Children

AirPlot is intended for adults involved in flight planning and related tasks. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights (UK and EEA)

If UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies to processing we carry out (for example emails you send us), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Contact support@airplot.app for requests relating to information we hold.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site or practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when we do.

Disclaimer

Legal limitations and operational disclaimers for use of the app are also set out in the Disclaimer tab inside Help on the main planning map. Please read that alongside this policy.
