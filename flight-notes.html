Privacy: AirPlot does not save your flight report on our servers. What you enter stays in this browser on this device and is not uploaded to us. Your draft is saved locally so you can leave this page and return later; use Clear report at the top of the page or Clear form at the bottom to wipe it from this browser. For a portable copy, use export to PDF or send by email. Third-party services are contacted only when you use an optional feature that needs them to fetch information for your location (for example forecast data for Fetch weather report, map tiles for the preview map, or coordinates from Search location / postcode lookup). Your typed report text is not sent to those services.
