Flight Report

ℹ️ Privacy info

Privacy: AirPlot does not save your flight report on our servers. What you enter stays in this browser on this device and is not uploaded to us. Your draft is saved locally so you can leave this page and return later; use Clear Report next to the page title or at the bottom to wipe it from this browser. For a portable copy, use export to PDF or send by email. Third-party services are contacted only when you use an optional feature that needs them to fetch information for your location (for example forecast data for Fetch Weather Report, map tiles for the preview map, or coordinates from Search location / postcode lookup). Your typed report text is not sent to those services.

Record key details from your flight. The sun/moon button (top right) toggles this page only. At the bottom, PDF appearance next to Export PDF chooses light or dark for the exported PDF only (default Light). Either Clear Report control (beside the title or at the bottom) wipes the draft after confirmation. Use Fetch Weather Report and Clear Weather Report (Conditions) and the Airspace section (NOTAMs and UK zones with small maps) when you have a location. Export to PDF or send by email. The header tool strip switches to Checklist, Planner, Weather, or the live Airspace map in this tab without returning to the welcome screen.
